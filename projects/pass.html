PASS: Portable Assisted Standing System

A portable seat that sits on most chairs and, with the pull of a lever, mechanically lifts the user at an angle so they can stand up without help. We presented it at the Jacobs Design Showcase.

Team
Daniela Uyuni, Julian Bourret, Wen Cao, Jackson Christopher
Video
Watch the demo

The problem

People with limited lower-body mobility often struggle to stand up from a chair on their own, and many need someone nearby to help.

The idea

Rather than a whole new chair, PASS is a device you place on the chair you already have. The user sits down, pulls a lever, and the seat lifts them at an angle that takes pressure off their legs and makes it easy to stand.

How it compares

We looked at two kinds of existing products. Powered lift cushions work, but they are expensive and harder to operate. Standing handles are cheap and simple, but they do not actually lift the user. PASS aims to be effective, affordable, portable, and easy to use all at once.

Building it

We dimensioned and toleranced our custom parts, including the seat connector, and machined them ourselves.

Why it matters

Because it fits most chairs, PASS can help many different people in many different places, and its parts are simple enough to manufacture cheaply at scale.
