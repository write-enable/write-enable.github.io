Sugar-control chopsticks

Chopsticks with a small camera module that estimates the sugar content of food and gives instant feedback, paired with an app for tracking what you eat. I designed them to help people with or at risk of diabetes build healthier habits.

Role
Independent project: research, product design, prototype, and app
Patent
#202023061241.7
Survey
100 respondents

Why I built it

My uncle, a taxi driver, was diagnosed with type 2 diabetes linked to his diet and long hours sitting in his car. His story led me to the bigger picture: about 422 million people worldwide have diabetes, and diets heavy in refined carbohydrates like rice and noodles drive fast spikes in blood sugar.

The goals were to make tracking sugar intake effortless, to build healthier eating habits into daily life, and to raise awareness of diabetes.

Research

I ran a cross-sectional survey of 100 people about their demographics, diet, and exercise. More than half expressed concern about their weight, 10% reported obesity, and almost half had sweets or sugary drinks more than three times a week, with 9% having them every day. About half cooked at home or ate at a dining hall, while the rest mostly ordered delivery or ate out.

From idea to design

Early ideas included a food-detecting camera mounted on glasses. I settled on chopsticks because they are already part of every meal for my target users, so the device adds no new habit. The module connects to a phone app that logs meals and offers advice.

The module holds a camera, battery, and Wi-Fi chip that sends what the camera sees to the app for food identification. Indicator lights show red for high sugar, orange for medium, and yellow for low, and green for foods that help lower blood sugar. A privacy light turns on whenever the camera is active.

For the look, I drew on the Taotie, a gluttonous creature from ancient mythology said to have eaten its own body, as a gentle warning about overeating.

Prototype

The app

The companion app shows the sugar level of each detected food in color, keeps a diet history with nutrient values, offers a search across meals and nutrition data, and gives diet tips.
