IoT smart pillbox

A compact, travel-friendly pillbox that reminds you when to take each medication, watches the temperature and humidity your pills are stored in, and opens only the compartment you need.

Team
Wen Cao, Emily Harris, Abdel Zaro
Hardware
ESP32, DC motor, temperature and humidity sensor, LEDs, buzzer
Video
Watch the demo

The gap

Managing several medications a day is hard. Existing pill organizers tend to be either simple but not smart, or smart but expensive and tied to a monthly subscription. We wanted something simple, useful, and affordable.

Reminders

The ESP32 keeps track of the real time over Wi-Fi. When a dose is due, say 4:00 PM, the LED for that compartment lights up and blinks, and an email reminder goes to the user’s phone. Each LED is tied to one compartment, so it is obvious which pills to take.

Storage monitoring

A sensor logs the temperature and humidity inside the box every 30 minutes. If either goes out of a safe range, a buzzer, amplified with an op-amp, sounds and an email alert goes out. Caretakers or doctors can check live readings on an Adafruit IO dashboard.

Opening the right compartment

A compartment opens only when its button is pressed: a motor turns a disk to the right lid, and torsion springs pop it open. We made this a local action instead of controlling it over the internet, so the box still works when the Wi-Fi does not, which was a real concern on campus.
